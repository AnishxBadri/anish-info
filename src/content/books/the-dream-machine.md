I picked this up expecting a history lesson and got a design brief instead. Licklider never wrote much code, yet he funded and connected the people who did, and he kept asking one question: what if computers helped people think, not just calculate?

The part that stayed with me is how patient that vision was. Decades between the idea and the thing. It made me less anxious about how slowly my own projects move, and more careful about which questions I let them answer.
